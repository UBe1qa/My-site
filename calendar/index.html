Questions

Are there ads or logos inside the file?

No. The PDF, the image and the printout carry no ads. Only the site address is printed in very small type in the bottom corner.

Why is December 31, 2027 marked as a holiday?

New Year’s Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so the federal holiday is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027. The calendar shows observed days because those are the days offices actually close.

Can I print on A4 instead of Letter?

Yes. Open “Change settings” and pick A4. The preview changes shape and the PDF and print layout follow it.

Can weeks start on Monday?

Yes. Choose Monday under “Change settings”. With week numbers on, Monday-start calendars use ISO 8601 week numbers and Sunday-start calendars count from the week that contains January 1.

Is the PDF real text or a picture?

Real text. Every PDF, whatever settings you pick, keeps its letters as letters, so it prints sharply at any size and the text can be searched.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. “Download PDF” saves the file directly. Tap the preview to enlarge it.
