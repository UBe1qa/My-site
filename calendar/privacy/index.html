Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Operated by Lumen Lab

What you enter and the files you make

Onesheet builds calendars inside your browser. The year and settings you choose and the PDF and image files it creates are never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts.

What is stored on your device

If you close the language suggestion bar or follow a language link, that choice is saved in your browser’s localStorage under the key cal.lang so the bar does not come back. Clearing site data removes it. Calendar settings are not stored.

Ads

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and other ad vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Because of these ads, third parties such as Google may place or read cookies in your browser and collect information through web beacons (tiny invisible images). See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings. We do not use separate analytics tools. The files you download and the pages you print contain no ads.

Fonts and server logs

The page loads its font from jsDelivr, a public content network, which receives your IP address like any web request. The site is served by Cloudflare, which may briefly keep basic request logs such as IP addresses to run the service securely.
