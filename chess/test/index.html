      How the test works

      1. Every position comes from the opening map: real Lichess games, counted by rating.
      2. The right answer is the move that 2000+ players choose most in that position. The other three are moves people play at lower ratings.
      3. Your level comes from how many you get right. The rating line compares your picks with how often each rating group plays them.
      4. Nothing is sent anywhere: the test runs in your browser.
