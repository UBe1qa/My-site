    About Opening Trail

    Opening Trail lets you walk every chess opening from the first move, like a trail: why each move is played, how players at your rating really answer, and which popular moves are actually bad. It is free and made by Lumen Lab.

    Who it is for

    Anyone who plays chess. If you do not like studying openings, read the one-line summary and follow the main line. If you want depth, every move also has a note for stronger players, an engine score and how often masters choose it.

    What it is built from

    We counted 174,931,775 rated Lichess games from August to October 2026 ourselves. Engine scores come from the Lichess evaluation database, and the master numbers from Lichess broadcasts of real events. The method and licenses are on Data and sources.

    Who writes the text

    Guides and move reasons are written by Claude (AI), and every line is played move by move with a chess rules library each time the site is built. Each section says whether it was written by AI, checked with the engine, or read by a person. If you spot a mistake, write to contact@lumenlab.page.
