Plans for both sides

White

  • Against 1...d5, 2.Bg2 attacks d5; after 2...Bxg4 3.c4 the pressure on the long diagonal is worth the pawn.
  • Against 1...e5, Bg2, d3 and h3 keep the g-pawn safe; never play f3 or f4 while ...Qh4 is possible.
  • Qb3 often joins the attack on b7.

Black

  • Take the center with 1...d5 or 1...e5 and develop quickly.
  • After 1...d5 2.Bg2, 2...c6 is the safe answer: it holds d5 and blunts the g2 bishop.
  • Look for ...Qh4 and ...h5: the g4 pawn and f2 are White’s weak points.

Common traps

1...e5 2.f4?? Qh4#

The fastest possible mate against White: after 1.g4 e5 2.f4?? the e1–h4 diagonal is wide open and 2...Qh4 is mate. With the g-pawn gone, the f-pawn must stay home.

Fewer than 6 games in our data reached the end of this line.

At club level

Below 1200 White scores only about 46% with 1.g4; at 2000+ about 51%. After the Grob Gambit 1...d5 2.Bg2 Bxg4 3.c4, White scores about 59%, so taking the pawn is risky. At 2000+ about three in ten answer 2.Bg2 with the safe 2...c6.
