Plans for both sides

White

  • Play Bf4 early, before e3 shuts the bishop in.
  • The usual setup: e3, Nf3, c3, Bd3, Nbd2, O-O, then Ne5 or e4.
  • Against ...c5 and ...Qb6, defend b2 with Qb3 or Qc1.

Black

  • Hit d4 with ...c5 and the b2 pawn with ...Qb6; this is the most annoying plan for White.
  • Trade the f4 bishop with ...Bd6 or ...Nh5.
  • A King’s Indian setup (...g6, ...d6, ...e5) avoids the London’s comfort zone.

At club level

The London is everywhere below 2000. As White, the setup is easy, but know the ...c5–...Qb6 plan. As Black, that same plan is the best way to make a London player think.
