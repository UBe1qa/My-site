Plans for both sides

White

  • Réti: c4 and g3 attack d5 from the side; Bg2 rakes the long diagonal.
  • King’s Indian Attack: g3, Bg2, O-O, d3, Nbd2 and e4 against almost anything.
  • With d4 you can reach a London, Queen’s Gambit or Catalan on your own terms.

Black

  • 1...d5 and 1...Nf6 are the most flexible replies.
  • Against the Réti, ...d4 grabs space; ...c6 and ...Bf5 or ...Bg4 is a solid setup.
  • ...c5 and ...Nc6 can lead to a Sicilian-style game if White plays e4.
