Plans for both sides

White

  • Nc3, Bg5 (or Bf4), e3 and Nf3, then Bd3 and O-O.
  • The Exchange (cxd5 exd5) gives a clear plan: the minority attack b4–b5.
  • Do not fall for the Elephant trap: the d5 pawn is not always free.

Black

  • Develop ...Nf6, ...Be7, ...O-O and ...Nbd7; the c8 bishop is the problem piece.
  • Free the game with ...c5 or ...e5, or trade pieces with ...Ne4 or ...dxc4 and ...c5.
  • The Tarrasch (...c5 early) and Ragozin (...Bb4) are more active choices.

Common traps

The Elephant trap

With the knight on d7, the f6 knight looks pinned and the d5 pawn looks free. 6.Nxd5?? Nxd5! 7.Bxd8 Bb4+ 8.Qd2 Bxd2+ 9.Kxd2 Kxd8 and Black is a piece up.

Fewer than 6 games in our data reached the end of this line.
