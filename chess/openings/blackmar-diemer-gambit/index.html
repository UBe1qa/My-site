Plans for both sides

White

  • Develop fast: Nxf3, Bd3 or Bc4, O-O, then Qe1 and Qh4 or Qg3 to bring everything toward the king.
  • The half-open f-file and the f7 square are your targets; Rxf6 sacrifices are a common idea.
  • Against side lines like the Lemberger 3...e5 or 4...Bf5, keep developing rather than chasing material.

Black

  • 5...Bg4 (Teichmann) is the most common answer, and 5...g6 (Bogoljubow) scores best for Black here, with White at only about 46%.
  • 5...e6 (Euwe) is natural but gives White the attack it wants: White scores about 60% after it.
  • Declining with 2...e6 (a French) or 2...c6 (a Caro-Kann) avoids the gambit altogether.

At club level

At every level about seven in ten accept the pawn with 2...dxe4. After 5.Nxf3, 5...Bg4 is the most common answer and 5...e6 is next, but White scores about 60% against 5...e6 and only about 46% against 5...g6.
