Plans for both sides

White

  • 4.Bb5 is the solid main line; 4.d4 opens the center.
  • 4.Bc4 lets Black play the fork trick 4...Nxe4!, so know what you are getting into.
  • The Halloween Gambit 4.Nxe5 is a club favorite: a piece for two pawns and a big center.

Black

  • Against 4.Bb5, 4...Bb4 (symmetrical) or 4...Nd4 (Rubinstein) are both fine.
  • Against 4.Bc4, the fork trick 4...Nxe4 5.Nxe4 d5 equalizes at once.
  • Against 4.d4, 4...exd4 5.Nxd4 Bb4 is the simplest.
