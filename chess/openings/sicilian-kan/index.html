Sicilian Kan (4...a6): Maróczy Bind, 5.Bd3 and Stats by Rating

4...a6 is the most flexible move in the Sicilian. It takes b5 away from White’s pieces and keeps every option open: ...Qc7, ...b5, ...Nf6, ...Bc5 or ...d6. Black waits to see White’s setup before choosing a structure.

Plans for both sides

White

  • 5.Nc3 is the most common move; after 5...Qc7 develop with Bd3 or Be3 and castle.
  • 5.Bd3 covers e4 with a piece and keeps the c-pawn free to go to c4.
  • 5.c4 is the Maróczy Bind: pawns on c4 and e4 grip d5 and leave Black cramped.

Black

  • ...Qc7 covers e5 and c5. Against 5.Nc3 it is the most common reply at every rating.
  • Against 5.c4 play the Hedgehog: ...Nf6, ...Qc7, ...b6, ...Bb7 and ...d6, then wait for ...b5 or ...d5.
  • Against 5.Bd3, ...Bc5 or ...Nf6 with ...Qc7 develops quickly.

At club level

The Kan scores well for Black: White averages only about 48% after 4...a6, and about 46% between 1600 and 1999. At 2000+ the Maróczy Bind 5.c4 is White’s second choice (27%), so learn the Hedgehog setup before you play the Kan against strong players.
