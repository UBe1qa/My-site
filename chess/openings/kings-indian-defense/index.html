Plans for both sides

White

  • Classical: Nf3, Be2, O-O; after ...e5 and d5, push c5 on the queenside.
  • Sämisch: f3 and Be3, often castling long and attacking with g4 and h4.
  • Do not open the center while Black’s pieces are aimed at your king.

Black

  • ...O-O, ...e5, then ...Nc6 or ...Na6; after d5, play ...Ne8 and ...f5.
  • The kingside pawn storm (...f5–f4, ...g5) is the main plan.
  • The g7 bishop is your best piece; do not trade it cheaply.

At club level

Club King’s Indian games are races: Black attacks on the kingside and White on the queenside. Learning the ...f5 plan is worth more than memorizing move orders.
