Plans for both sides

White

  • After 3...Bd7 4.Bxd7+ Qxd7, castle and play c4 (a Maróczy-style bind) or c3 and d4.
  • After 3...Nd7, keep the bishop: O-O, d4 or c3, and Re1 give you a lead in development.
  • After 3...Nc6 you are in a Rossolimo with ...d6: O-O and c3 with d4, or Bxc6+ to double the pawns.

Black

  • 3...Bd7 is simple and safe: trade bishops and develop with ...Nc6, ...Nf6 and ...g6 or ...e6.
  • 3...Nd7 keeps more pieces on and is the choice of many strong players; watch out for an early d4 and e5.
  • After 4.Bxd7+, taking back with the queen leaves c6 free for the knight.

At club level

More than half the players at every rating answer 3.Bb5+ with 3...Bd7, and at 2000+ 3...Nd7 rises to 36%. After 4.Bxd7+ Qxd7, six in ten 2000+ players castle and three in ten play 5.c4.
