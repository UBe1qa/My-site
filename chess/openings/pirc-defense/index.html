    For stronger players

    Black lets White build a big center and plans to attack it later with ...c5 or ...e5. The bishop on g7 is the key piece. It is flexible and avoids theory, but White gets free attacking chances.

    Below 2000 the most common answer to 3...g6 is the calm 4.Nf3, with 4.Bg5 second. At 2000+ the choices are spread between 4.f4 (20%), 4.Nf3, 4.Be3 and 4.Bg5. White scores about 54% after 4.Be3, so learn when to delay castling against the Be3–Qd2–Bh6 plan.

    What now: plans for both sides

    White

    • The Austrian Attack (f4) grabs maximum space; the 150 Attack (Be3, Qd2, Bh6) aims at the king.
    • The Classical (Nf3, Be2, O-O) is calm and keeps a small edge.
    • Trade Black’s g7 bishop with Bh6 when you castle long.

    Black

    • Strike the center with ...e5 or ...c5 once you have castled.
    • Against Be3 and Qd2, do not castle into an attack without ...c6 and ...b5 counterplay.
    • The Modern Defense (...g6 without ...Nf6) keeps even more options open and has its own guide.
