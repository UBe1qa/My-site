Plans for both sides

White

  • The Yugoslav Attack (Be3, f3, Qd2, O-O-O, h4–h5) is the most dangerous plan.
  • Trade the g7 bishop with Bh6 when you can; the Dragon is much weaker without it.
  • Quieter lines with Be2 and O-O are easier to play but less testing.

Black

  • Castle short, put a rook on c8, and use ...Ne5–c4 to fight White’s bishop.
  • The exchange sacrifice ...Rxc3 is a key idea to break White’s queenside.
  • The Accelerated Dragon (...Nc6 and ...g6 without ...d6) aims for ...d5 in one go.

Common traps

6.Be3 Ng4? 7.Bb5+

6...Ng4 hits the e3 bishop, but 7.Bb5+! wins a piece: after 7...Bd7 8.Qxg4 Bxb5 9.Ndxb5 White is a knight up. Black should play 6...Bg7 first.
