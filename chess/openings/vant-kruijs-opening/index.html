Van’t Kruijs Opening (1.e3): Setups and Stats by Rating

1.e3 opens the f1 bishop and the queen’s diagonal but takes no central square. It often transposes: with d4 later it becomes a quiet 1.d4 system, with b3 a Nimzo-Larsen, with c4 an English. It is especially common below 1200, where nearly three in ten of the 1.e3 games here are played.

Plans for both sides

White

  • Follow with d4 for a Colle- or London-like setup, or b3 and Bb2 for a Nimzo-Larsen.
  • Against 1...e5, 2.d4 hits the center at once and is the most common move at every level.
  • An early Qh5 or Qf3 with Bc4 sets traps but is easy to meet with ...Nc6, ...g6 or ...Qe7.

Black

  • Take the center: 1...e5 and 1...d5 are the most common and the most natural.
  • After 1...e5 2.d4, 2...exd4 3.exd4 d5 gives a symmetrical, easy game.
  • Watch f7 against early Qh5 and Bc4 ideas.

At club level

Below 1200, about half the players answer 1.e3 with 1...e5, and 2.Qh5 is then White’s second most common move. At 2000+ replies spread out, and after 1...e5 2.d4 leads at 38%. White scores about 50% after 1.e3 at every level.
