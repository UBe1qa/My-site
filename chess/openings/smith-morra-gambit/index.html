Plans for both sides

White

  • After 3...dxc3 4.Nxc3, aim the pieces at d6 and f7: Bc4, Nf3, O-O, Qe2 and Rd1.
  • The open c- and d-files are your compensation; use them before Black catches up.
  • If Black declines with 3...Nf6 or 3...d5, you get an Alapin-style game.

Black

  • Accepting is fine; develop with ...Nc6, ...e6, ...a6 and ...Nge7 or ...Nf6, and castle.
  • Declining with 3...Nf6 4.e5 Nd5 or 3...d5 avoids all the theory.
  • Watch out for Nb5 and Nd5 jumps and the Siberian trap (below).

Common traps

The Siberian trap

In this common setup, 9.h3?? walks into 9...Nd4!: the knight hits the queen, and 10.Nxd4 allows 10...Qh2 mate. Before you play h3 here, look at what the g4 knight and the c7 queen do to h2.
