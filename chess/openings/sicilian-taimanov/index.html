Plans for both sides

White

  • 5.Nc3 is the main move. Against 5...Qc7 the English Attack with Be3, Qd2 and O-O-O is the sharpest plan.
  • 5.Nb5 hits d6 and asks Black for ...d6, a Scheveningen-like structure where c4 clamps the center.
  • 5.Nxc6 bxc6 is simple but helps Black: the b-pawn comes to c6 and backs up ...d5.

Black

  • 5...Qc7 or 5...a6 keeps the knight off b5 and prepares ...Nf6 and ...Bb4.
  • 5...Nf6 is the Four Knights. After 6.Ndb5 play 6...Bb4 or 6...d6; 6.Nxc6 bxc6 7.e5 Nd5 is the other main line.
  • The ...d5 break is your main freeing move; prepare it with ...Nf6 and ...Bb4.

At club level

Below 1200 half the players answer 4...Nc6 with 5.Nxc6, and between 1200 and 1599 it is still just ahead of 5.Nc3 (37% to 36%). After 5.Nxc6 bxc6 White scores only about 48%. At 2000+ six in ten play 5.Nc3.
