Plans for both sides

White

  • 2.Nf3 and 3.d4 is the Open Sicilian: more space and attacking chances, but a lot of theory.
  • 2.c3 (Alapin) and 2.Nc3 (Closed) avoid the theory and still give a good game.
  • In the Open Sicilian White often castles long and pushes the kingside pawns.

Black

  • After 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 you have a central pawn majority and the half-open c-file.
  • Choose one system against the Open Sicilian (...d6, ...Nc6 or ...e6) and learn its pawn structure.
  • Counterplay comes on the queenside: ...a6, ...b5 and pressure on c3.
