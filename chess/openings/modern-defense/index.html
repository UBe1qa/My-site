Modern Defense (1...g6): Lines, Plans and Stats by Rating

1...g6 lets White build any center and then attacks it from the side. The g7 bishop hits d4 and the pawns stay flexible, so the same setup works against almost anything. The risk is that White simply takes too much space.

Plans for both sides

White

  • 3.Nc3 d6 4.Be3 with Qd2, f3 and O-O-O is the most popular plan at 2000+: trade the g7 bishop with Bh6 and attack.
  • 3.Nf3 with Be2 or Bc4 and O-O is a quiet, solid setup.
  • 3.f4 (Three Pawns Attack) grabs the most space but needs care: Black hits the center with ...c5 or ...d5.

Black

  • Usual setup: ...d6 with ...a6 and ...b5, or ...Nd7 and ...c5. Adding ...Nf6 turns it into a Pirc.
  • Strike the center with ...c5 or ...e5 once your pieces are out; do not just wait.
  • The Hippopotamus (...g6, ...b6, ...Bb7, ...Bg7, ...d6, ...e6, ...Nd7, ...Ne7) is a slow wall that works best against players who overpress.

At club level

Below 1600 the most common answer to 2...Bg7 is 3.Nf3, and below 1200 three in ten push 3.e5. At 2000+ 3.Nc3 leads (36%), and after 3...d6 the top choice is 4.Be3 (42%). White scores about 54% after 3.Nc3 d6.
