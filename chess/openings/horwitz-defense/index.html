Horwitz Defense (1...e6): English Defense, Kangaroo and Stats

1...e6 keeps Black’s options open: after 2.e4 it is a French, after 2.c4 d5 a Queen’s Gambit Declined, and after 2.c4 f5 a Dutch. The lines that stay independent are the English Defense with ...b6 and ...Bb7, and the Kangaroo with ...Bb4+.

Plans for both sides

White

  • 2.c4 is the most common move at 2000+; 2.e4 invites a French, and 2.Nf3 avoids the English Defense tricks.
  • Against the English Defense (2...b6), 3.Nc3 or 3.e4 takes the center; watch for ...Bb4 and ...Qh4 ideas.
  • Against the Kangaroo check 2...Bb4+, 3.Bd2 offers a trade and 3.Nc3 allows a Nimzo-like game.

Black

  • If you play the French, the Queen’s Gambit Declined or the Dutch, 1...e6 is a flexible move order.
  • The English Defense hits e4 with ...Bb7 and ...Bb4, and sometimes ...f5.
  • After 2.c4 Bb4+ 3.Bd2, trading with ...Bxd2+ leaves a solid, simple game.

At club level

Below 1200, 2.e4 and 2.Bf4 are the most common answers (about 23% each); at 2000+ 2.c4 leads at about 44%. The English Defense 2...b6 holds about even from 1200 up, but below 1200 White scores about 58% against it. The Kangaroo 2...Bb4+ scores about 56% for White overall.
