Plans for both sides

White

  • Nf3, Nc3 and e3 are the main moves; a4 stops ...b5 in the main line.
  • The Exchange Slav (cxd5 cxd5) is simple and drawish.
  • Against the Semi-Slav (...e6), be ready for sharp lines like the Meran.

Black

  • Play ...Nf6, then ...dxc4 and ...Bf5 (main Slav), or ...e6 (Semi-Slav).
  • In the Semi-Slav, ...dxc4 and ...b5 gain queenside space.
  • Keep the c8 bishop active: that is the whole point of ...c6.
