Plans for both sides

White

  • After 2...exf4, play 3.Nf3 to stop ...Qh4+ and prepare d4 and Bc4.
  • Aim at f7 with Bc4, castle, and use the half-open f-file.
  • If Black holds the pawn with ...g5, h4 breaks the chain.

Black

  • 2...exf4 is the critical answer; 3...d6, 3...g5 or 3...d5 are all good.
  • Declining with 2...Bc5 is safe and stops White from castling easily.
  • Your king is safe as long as you strike back in the center with ...d5 or ...d6.

Common traps

2...Bc5 3.fxe5??

Taking the pawn with 3.fxe5?? runs into 3...Qh4+: 4.Ke2 Qxe4 is mate, while 4.g3 Qxe4+ forks the king and the h1 rook. Play 3.Nf3 first.

Fewer than 6 games in our data reached the end of this line.
