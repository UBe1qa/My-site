Plans for both sides

White

  • Against 2...Nf6, 3.f4 (Vienna Gambit) is the sharpest; 3.Bc4 is calmer and sets a trap.
  • Against 2...Nc6, 3.Bc4 or 3.f4 again; g3 and Bg2 is a quiet option.
  • If you play f4, follow up with Nf3 and d3 before you open the f-file.

Black

  • 2...Nf6 is the main answer and hits e4. Meet 3.f4 with 3...d5!
  • 2...Nc6 and 2...Bc5 are simpler and avoid the sharpest lines after 2...Nf6 3.f4.
  • Do not let White get f4–f5 for free; ...d5 in the center is your best counter.

Common traps

3...Nxe4 4.Qh5 Nf6??

After 3...Nxe4 White hits f7 with 4.Qh5. The natural-looking 4...Nf6?? attacks the queen but allows 5.Qxf7 mate. Black must play 4...Nd6.

Fewer than 6 games in our data reached the end of this line.
