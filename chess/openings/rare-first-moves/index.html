    For stronger players

    Every first move has a name. These are rare because they do little for the center or development, but none of them loses by force. Against all of them the simple recipe works: take the center with ...e5 or ...d5, develop and castle.

    Below 1200 every move on this page scores under 50% for White, from about 37% with 1.Na3 to about 49% with 1.c3. At 2000+ most of them score over 50%, and 1.h3 scores about 59%: strong players who choose these moves know what they are doing. 1.c3 is the most played of them, with about 38,000 games here.

    What now: plans for both sides

    White

    • 1.c3 and 1.a3 are the most useful: they can turn into a reversed Caro-Kann, a 1.d4 system or an English with a3.
    • 1.f3 (the Barnes) loosens the e1–h4 diagonal; after 1...e5 avoid g4 and Kf2 adventures.
    • After wing moves like 1.a4, 1.h4 and 1.h3, continue with a normal setup such as d4, Nf3 and e3.

    Black

    • 1...e5 or 1...d5 and quick development is enough against every move here.
    • Do not copy White’s odd move; use the extra time in the center.
    • Against 1.f3, ...e5 and ...d5 build a big center, and ...Qh4+ ideas appear if White loosens g3.
