King’s Indian Attack (KIA): Setup, Plans and Stats by Rating

The King’s Indian Attack is a setup more than a line: Nf3, g3, Bg2, O-O, d3, Nbd2 and e4, played against almost anything. It is the King’s Indian Defense with colors reversed and an extra move, and White often follows with e5 and a kingside attack.

Plans for both sides

White

  • Build the setup first; the e4 push, and later e5, decide the plan.
  • Against ...e6 and ...c5 (French or Sicilian setups), e5 gains space, and Re1, Nf1, h4 and Bf4 build the kingside attack.
  • Against ...c6 with ...Bg4 or ...Bf5, play h3 or e4 to ask the bishop where it is going.

Black

  • ...d5, ...Nf6, ...c5 and ...Nc6 claim space; queenside play with ...b5–b4 races White’s kingside attack.
  • When you can, develop the c8 bishop before ...e6: ...Bg4 or ...Bf5 suits this setup.
  • If White plays e5, hit the pawn chain with ...f6 or ...c5–c4.

At club level

White scores about 54% after 1.Nf3 d5 2.g3, and about 55% at 2000+, better than most first-move setups. Below 1200 the most common reply is 2...Nc6, at 2000+ 2...Nf6. Against the French (1.e4 e6 2.d3 d5 3.Nd2) White also scores about 55%.
