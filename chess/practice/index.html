How the trainer works

  1. Your moves follow the guide’s main line. After that, the trainer expects the move that players rated 2000+ choose most often in that position.
  2. Your opponent plays the replies that people at the rating you picked really play, in the same proportions. Popular replies come up often; rare ones do not waste your time.
  3. A wrong move shows the right one with a green arrow and saves the position. It comes back the same day, then after 1, 3, 7, 16, 35 and 90 days as you get it right.
  4. If you play a different move that strong players also choose often, it does not count as wrong. You are asked to find the move this line trains.
