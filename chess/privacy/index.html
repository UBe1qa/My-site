Privacy Policy

Last checked: November 4, 2026

What this site keeps on your device

So your rating choice and practice progress are there next time, they are saved in this browser's storage (localStorage) only. Nothing is sent to a server. These are the three keys:

  • fm.band: the rating level you picked
  • fm.cards: for each practiced position, the next review date, the interval, right and wrong counts, and the moves that led there
  • fm.lang: whether you closed the language suggestion

Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes all of it. There are no accounts or logins.

What the site loads

Move statistics are read from files on this site (chess.lumenlab.page). The moves you play on the board are not sent anywhere. The font is loaded from jsDelivr.

Visit statistics

Cloudflare, which hosts the site, adds a small script for visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). It collects things like the page address, the previous page address, how long the page took to load and the browser type, and it does not use cookies. The moves you play and your practice progress are not part of it.
