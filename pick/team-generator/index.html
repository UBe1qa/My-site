    How to use it

    Choose Number of teams or Max per team, set the number and press Make teams. The list is shuffled and dealt out, so the first teams get any extra person and no team is more than one person larger than another.

    With Max per team, the number of teams is the list size divided by that maximum, rounded up. Thirteen people with a maximum of four gives four teams of 4, 3, 3 and 3.

    What it does not do

    Teams are random. The tool does not balance skill, keep certain people together or keep them apart. There are no hidden rules that place a particular person in a particular team.

    Questions

    What happens when the list does not divide evenly?

    The extra people are spread one each over the first teams, so sizes differ by one at most.

    Can I send the teams to a group chat?

    Use Copy as text to paste the teams as plain text, or Copy result link to send an address that shows the same teams again.
