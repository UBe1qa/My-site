Dice roller

Roll one die or up to 100, with 2 to 1,000 sides.

Choose your dice and press Roll.

The link holds your settings and the value this result was made from.

How does it pick? Each press uses fresh random numbers from your browser. What you enter is not sent to a server.

Details

Each press asks your browser for a new 32-byte value, the seed, with crypto.getRandomValues(). A fixed calculation called xoshiro256** then turns that seed into the numbers the pick needs. That is why the same seed and the same names give the same result on anyone’s device. See exactly how picks are made

Recent results on this device

    How to use it

    Choose how many dice (up to 100) and how many sides (from 2 to 1,000), then press Roll. Each die is shown with its value and the total appears above them. Six-sided dice are drawn with pips; other dice show the number.

    How the roll is made

    Every die is an independent pick from 1 to the number of sides, each value equally likely, starting from a fresh value your browser gives on every press. Automated tests roll 100,000 six-sided and twenty-sided dice with fixed seeds and check that the faces come up evenly.

    Questions

    Can I roll dice with an unusual number of sides?

    Yes. Type any number of sides from 2 to 1,000 instead of choosing from the usual ones.
