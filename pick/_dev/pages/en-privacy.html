What you type

Names, bottom-row labels, numbers and settings are processed inside your browser. They are not sent to any server, ours included. There are no accounts.

Result links

When you press Copy result link, the address that is created contains your list, the settings and the value the result was made from (the seed) after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part of an address to a server, so we never receive it. Anyone you give the link to can read the list in it, so share it only with people who may see those names.

What is stored on your device

The site saves the following in your browser’s localStorage, and nowhere else:

  • pick.list: the list you typed, so it is still there next time and on the other tools. People you take off the wheel with Remove and spin again are not saved.
  • pick.history: your last 30 results (tool, time, a short summary and the result link, which includes the list used; very long links are left out).
  • pick.sound: whether sound is on or off.
  • pick.lang: that you closed the language suggestion or chose a language, so the suggestion does not come back.

Clear list above the list removes pick.list. The Clear results too button that follows it, or Clear results under each tool, removes pick.history. The links in recent results contain the list, so on a shared computer clear both. Clearing site data in your browser removes everything.

Visit statistics

We look at visit statistics through Cloudflare Web Analytics: which pages were viewed and how fast they loaded. According to Cloudflare, it does not use cookies or local storage (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Nothing you type or load into the tools is included.

Ads

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and other ad vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Because of these ads, third parties such as Google may place or read cookies in your browser and collect information through web beacons (tiny invisible images). See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.

Fonts and server logs

The page first uses a subset font file served by this site, which holds only the characters the site itself displays. Separately, every page of this site loads one file from jsDelivr, a public content network: the stylesheet that lists the pieces of the Pretendard font. When a character outside the subset appears on screen, such as an unusual character in a name you enter, the font piece containing it is also loaded from jsDelivr. jsDelivr receives your IP address like any web request. The site is served by Cloudflare, which may briefly keep basic request logs such as IP addresses to run the service securely. Neither receives your list.
