Flip a coin

Heads or tails, once or many times.

Flip

The link holds your settings and the value this result was made from.

How does it pick? Each press uses fresh random numbers from your browser. What you enter is not sent to a server.

Details

Each press asks your browser for a new 32-byte value, the seed, with crypto.getRandomValues(). A fixed calculation called xoshiro256** then turns that seed into the numbers the pick needs. That is why the same seed and the same names give the same result on anyone’s device. See exactly how picks are made

Recent results on this device

    How to use it

    Press Flip. For several flips at once, set the number first, up to 1,000. The last flip is shown on the coin and the count of heads and tails appears below. Up to 300 flips, every flip is also listed in order.

    Is it 50/50?

    Each flip is one random bit. The bits come from a fresh value your browser gives on every press, so heads and tails are equally likely and one flip does not affect the next. Streaks still happen. In 10 fair flips, a run of three or more of the same side is more likely than not.

    Questions

    Why did I get the same side five times in a row?

    That is normal. For any five fair flips, the chance that all five land on the same side is 1 in 16 (1 in 32 for all heads plus 1 in 32 for all tails), so it turns up regularly over many flips. Each new flip is still 50/50.
