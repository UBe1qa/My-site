Questions

When and where can I get it?

Android (Google Play) comes first. The game is being play-tested on phones now, and there is no release date yet.

Will it work on iPhone?

It is in the works. An iPhone build (iOS 15 or later) exists but has not been checked on a real iPhone yet.

Do I have to pay? What about ads?

All 1,440 main levels are free to the end. Under the current plan there are no ads between levels on your first day or your first 15 levels, and after that at most one every 3 levels. Removing ads and the puzzle packs are paid, with prices set at launch. There are no paid random draws.

I’m not great at puzzles. Can I still play?

Yes. Start with Classic, which has no abilities. Once no tube is mixed, the game finishes the tidy-up for you.
