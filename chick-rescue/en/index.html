Twelve friends, twelve abilities

Hold a friend to use its ability. Some levels need it.

Squirrel

Hold to climb straight to the top. The critters above move down one space.

Every Abilities level with a friend in it needs that ability to solve. In Abilities and Rescue, stages 1 to 4 each bring three new friends.

When you get stuck

Built so you never stay stuck

First hint free on every level

It shows your next move on the board, like the Hint button in the level above.

Undo and restart are always free

Under the current plan, use them as often as you like with no ads and no cost.

Back to just before you got stuck

If the board can no longer be solved, the game tells you and takes you back in one tap.

The tidy-up is done for you

Once no tube is mixed, the game finishes merging the colors itself.

New to sort puzzles? Start with Classic, which has no abilities.

Cost

All 1,440 main levels are free

Ads and in-app purchases are not switched on yet. Here are the rules already built in.

Free

  • All 1,440 main levels
  • The Daily Challenge
  • The first stage of all 6 puzzle packs
  • The first hint on every level, plus undo and restart

Ads

  • No ads between levels on your first day or your first 15 levels
  • After that, at most one every 3 levels and never within 3 minutes
  • Watch a short ad for an extra hint or stars, only if you want to

If you pay

  • Remove ads
  • Puzzle packs from stage 2 on (840 levels)
  • Past Daily Challenges
  • Hint bundles
Prices are set at launch

There are no paid random draws. Since the game is not out yet, these rules may still change.

Questions

When and where can I get it?

Android (Google Play) comes first. The game is being play-tested on phones now, and there is no release date yet.

Will it work on iPhone?

It is in the works. An iPhone build (iOS 15 or later) exists but has not been checked on a real iPhone yet.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game runs without an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. There are no accounts and no cloud saves.

Does it collect my data?

The current game collects and sends nothing. If ads are added at launch, we will publish a privacy policy on this site first, saying exactly what is collected.

Which languages?

Korean, English and Japanese.

Any scary scenes or loot boxes?

No. There is no fighting or scary content, and no paid random draws. The age rating will be set at launch.
