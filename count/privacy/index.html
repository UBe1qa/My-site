Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · Operated by Lumen Lab

The text you type or paste

Kankan counts text inside your browser. This site does not send the text you type or paste to a server and does not collect it. It is gone when you close or reload the page. There are no accounts.

What is stored on your device

Only the following items are kept in your browser's localStorage. Clearing site data removes them.

  • kk.lang: a note that you closed or followed the language suggestion bar, so that it does not come back.
  • kk.jasoseo.on, kk.jasoseo: created only if you turn on “save on this device” on the Korean cover-letter counter. They hold the question names, goals, your text and the line-break setting, and are deleted as soon as you turn the switch off or press the delete button there.

Nothing else is stored.

Ads

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and other ad vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Because of these ads, third parties such as Google may place or read cookies in your browser and collect information through web beacons (tiny invisible images). See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.

Visit statistics

We look at visit statistics through Cloudflare Web Analytics: which pages were viewed and how fast they loaded. According to Cloudflare, it does not use cookies or local storage. Nothing you type or paste into the tools is included.

Fonts and server logs

The page loads its font from jsDelivr, a public content network, which receives your IP address like any web request. The site is served by Cloudflare, which may briefly keep basic request logs such as IP addresses to run the service securely. Neither ever receives your text.
