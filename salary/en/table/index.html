How this table was made

Every cell is calculated the same way as the net salary calculator. A salary of ₩30,000,000 leaves ₩2,244,440 a month, ₩40,000,000 leaves ₩2,935,813, ₩50,000,000 leaves ₩3,571,586 and ₩70,000,000 leaves ₩4,821,303.

  • Assumptions: one dependent (you), a ₩200,000 monthly non-taxable meal allowance, withholding at 100%, monthly pay = salary ÷ 12.
  • Rules as of October 2026: National Pension 4.75%, National Health Insurance 3.595%, Long-term Care Insurance at the health premium × 0.9448% ÷ 7.19%, Employment Insurance 0.9%, and the withholding table in force since 1 March 2026.
  • The income used for the National Pension stops at ₩6,590,000 a month, so the pension column stays the same above that.
  • More dependents, or children aged 8 to 20, lower the income tax. Click a row to open that salary in the calculator. It uses the standard monthly withholding table that applies to residents.
