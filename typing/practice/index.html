    Every finger has its own keys

    Rest your index fingers on F and J. Both keys have a small bump you can find without looking. The other fingers sit on the keys beside them, and each finger only reaches for the keys directly above and below its own. The on-screen keyboard lights the next key and names the finger under it.

    Five steps

    1. Home row A S D F G H J K L ;
    2. Top row Q W E R T Y U I O P
    3. Bottom row Z X C V B N M , .
    4. Shift capitals and ? ! : "
    5. All keys short sentences

    Each step uses only the new keys and the ones before them. Reach 95% accuracy or higher and the next step is suggested. You can also jump to any step.

    How the numbers are calculated

    Every number on this page is counted the way described here. Select a card to read more.

    WPM

    Correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ seconds × 60.

    One "word" is any five characters, spaces included. This is net WPM: only characters that are correct in the final text count.

    typing␣test=2.2 words11 characters ÷ 5
    Accuracy

    Correct key presses ÷ all key presses.

    A key is judged at the moment you press it, so a mistake you go back and fix still counts against accuracy.

    =90%
    Combo

    The number of keys you pressed correctly in a row.

    A wrong key starts it again from 0. Backspace neither adds to it nor breaks it, and keys typed while the wrong-layout or Caps Lock notice is showing do not break it. The result shows the longest run of that test. It has no effect on WPM or accuracy.

    5 combo→0
    Raw speedEvery key press ÷ 5 ÷ seconds × 60, including the ones you got wrong or erased.

    Backspace itself is not counted. It is shown in small print under the accuracy.

    Missed and slow keysA miss is recorded on the key you should have pressed.

    Typing "r" where "e" was expected counts against "e". A slow key is one whose average gap from the previous key is longer than your overall average. Pauses longer than 3 seconds and keys typed fewer than three times are left out.

    Wrong layout or Caps LockKey presses made while the "switch your keyboard" or Caps Lock notice is showing are not counted.

    They are left out of accuracy and missed keys, even when the notice stays up for several words.

    TimeThe clock starts on your first key and pauses when the tab is hidden.

    The time in the result is rounded to 0.1 s first, and WPM is calculated from that rounded time, so the formula on screen gives the same number if you redo it by hand.

    Touch keyboardsOn a phone we cannot see individual key presses, so we compare the text you have typed so far each time it changes.

    If you erase a character and type the same one again, it is counted once. Slow keys are not timed. Keyboard apps differ, so results can look different from one app to another.

    Other sitesTyping sites do not all count the same way.

    Here a character you leave wrong is left out of WPM and stays in accuracy, so a site that drops the whole word around a mistake will show a lower number. We do not claim our numbers match anyone else's.
