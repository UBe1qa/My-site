Questions people ask

Can QR codes be used for scams?

Yes. "Quishing" sends people to fake pages that ask for logins or payment. That is why this scanner never opens a link by itself and shows the real domain and warning signs first. If a code you found in public asks you to log in or pay, do not open it.

Which warning signs does it check?

Unencrypted http, link shorteners, IP addresses instead of a domain, punycode (xn--) or %-encoded domains that can disguise letters, an @ that hides the real domain, addresses dressed up to look like another site, words like login, verify or payment, and premium-rate numbers. No warning does not mean a link is safe.

Where does the camera image go?

Nowhere. The camera feed and uploaded images are read inside your browser and are not stored or sent.
