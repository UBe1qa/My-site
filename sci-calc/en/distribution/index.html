How to use

  1. Pick a distribution and a calculation at the top. Cumulative gives the probability of a range; density / probability gives the value at one point.
  2. For the normal distribution enter the mean μ and standard deviation σ. The standard normal has μ = 0 and σ = 1.
  3. Leave the lower bound empty for −∞ and the upper bound empty for +∞.
  4. Inverse normal finds the cut-off value from an area and its tail (left, right or center).

Example: with μ = 0 and σ = 1, P(−1.96 ≤ Z ≤ 1.96) ≈ 0.9500, and the inverse normal for a left area of 0.975 is about 1.959963985.
