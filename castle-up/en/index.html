Questions

When and where can I get it?

A Google Play (Android) launch is in the works. The game is being play-tested on phones now, and there is no release date yet.

Will it be on iPhone?

Not decided yet. Android is what is being prepared now.

Do I have to spend money? What about ads?

It is designed to be free to download. 2× speed, auto skills and auto replay are free too. Ads are designed as rewarded ads you only watch when you want, and recruiting heroes is a random draw (the odds are shown in the game).

Do I have to keep playing by hand?

No. Skills can fire on their own, and cleared waves replay automatically. Expeditions keep going while the game is closed. Only moving to the next wave needs a tap.
