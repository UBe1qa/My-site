Boss fights

A boss every 15 waves. Get the order right and it crumbles

Every boss shows a weakness order above its head. Skills fire on their own if you like, but aiming for the order yourself changes the fight.

Weakness order → stagger

Hit 2 to 4 elements in order and the boss is stunned for 4 seconds, taking 1.8× damage. A wrong element just passes with no penalty, and the button to press says ‘Next!’.

Parry

A boss’s wind-up ring turns gold for its last half second. Hold your skills and hit it then to cancel the attack and stagger it for 5 seconds.

Counter

Finish the order during a wind-up to cut the attack short and land a longer, bigger stagger.

Flyers fall

Flying bosses crash down while staggered, so ground-only skills reach them. Then they take off again.

14 element combos

When two skills’ elements pair up within 5 seconds, the second one hits 1.5 to 2× harder with a bonus effect.

Questions

When and where can I get it?

A Google Play (Android) launch is in the works. The game is being play-tested on phones now, and there is no release date yet.

Will it be on iPhone?

Not decided yet. Android comes first.

Do I have to keep playing by hand?

No. Skills can fire on their own, and cleared waves can replay automatically. Expeditions keep going while the game is closed. Only moving to the next wave needs a tap.

Where is my progress saved?

In 10 save slots on your phone. There are no accounts or cloud saves for now.

Does it collect my data?

The current game sends nothing anywhere. If ads or payments are added at launch, we will publish a privacy policy on this site first, saying exactly what is collected.

Which languages?

Korean, English and Japanese.

Is it scary?

It is fantasy combat in a bold-outline cartoon style, with fantasy foes like a Bone Shaman and a Demon Lord. The age rating will be set at launch.
