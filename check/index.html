What happens to your sound and video

The mic level, recordings, camera preview and photos are processed inside this tab. They are not uploaded, and they are gone when you close the tab.

The page asks for the mic or camera only after you press a button, and a Stop button stays visible while either is on.

Results are judged by rules shown next to them, for example what counts as double-typing. Where a number is an estimate, it says so.
