Chick Rescue

Puzzle game for phones · Android first

Getting ready for launch

What it is ↗

A sort puzzle: move animal friends between tubes, hold them to use abilities, and send the buried chicks home.

  • 1,440main levels, all free
  • 12friends with abilities
  • Offlineno account, saved on the phone

Preview

Real game screens. The game is still in development, so they may change before launch.

Both demo levels can be solved in 3 moves

  1. Tap twice to moveTap a tube, then an empty tube or one with the same critter on top, and the top critter moves over; each tube holds 4.
  2. Free the chickClear the critters sitting on the chick; once it reaches the top it flies to the nest, and a cleared level shows your moves and stars.
  3. Hold the squirrel‘Next: the squirrel’s ability’ opens the second level, where holding a squirrel sends it to the top of its tube.
  4. Use a hint when stuckPress ‘Hint’ to mark two tubes ‘From’ and ‘To’; ‘Undo’ takes back a move and ‘Restart’ starts the level over.

What we disclose

Screens and numbers reflect the game in development and may change before launch. The logo, backgrounds, nest tree and houses were made with an AI tool (Higgsfield); the animal friends and effects are drawn by the game’s own code. All 1,440 main levels stay free, and there are no paid random draws.

Privacy Policy →

Under the current plan, ads between levels come at most once every 3 levels

Chick Rescue rule sheet

Ads between levels
None on your first day or in your first 15 levels, then at most one every 3 levels. Removing ads is a paid option.Chick Rescue: Questions
Rewarded ads
Ads that give a hint or stars only play when you choose them, and ad content is limited to the PG rating.Chick Rescue Privacy Policy
What costs money
The 6 puzzle packs with 960 levels are paid, and the first stage of each pack is free.Chick Rescue site
Game progress
Cleared levels, stars, settings and purchased items stay on your phone and are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app or its data deletes them.Chick Rescue Privacy Policy
Payment details
The developer never receives card numbers or other payment details, and purchases are confirmed on your phone only.Chick Rescue Privacy Policy
Permissions and ad ID
The app does not use location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone permissions. Device data such as the advertising ID is collected directly by Google, which serves the ads, not by the developer.Chick Rescue Privacy Policy

The ad limits are given in the FAQ as the current plan.
