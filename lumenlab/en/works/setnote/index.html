SetNote

Opens in your browser · add it to your Home Screen

Available now

Open ↗

Save your routines and log every set with last time’s weight and reps beside it.

  • No sign‑upopens in your phone’s browser
  • Offlineonce on your Home Screen
  • On deviceyour log isn’t sent anywhere

Preview

Real app screens, taken after logging two sample workouts.

Repeating last time takes one tap per set

  1. Pick a suggested routineOn first open, press ‘Start now’ on ‘Push Day’, ‘Pull Day’ or ‘Leg Day’ and a workout opens with that routine.
  2. Log a setType the weight and reps in the ‘kg’ and ‘Reps’ boxes and tap the check on the right to log the set; an empty box takes the faded value shown in it.
  3. Time your restChecking a set starts the ‘Rest’ timer at 90 seconds by default; ‘−15’ and ‘+15’ change it by 15 seconds and ‘Skip’ ends it.
  4. Save and finishTap ‘Finish’ at the top, then ‘Finish’ again in the sheet to save; you get ‘Total volume’ and ‘Compared with last time’, and next time today's sets show under ‘Previous’.

What we disclose

Your log is saved on this device only and never sent to a server. To move it to another device, export and import a backup file. The screens above are the real app after logging two sample workouts.

What it does →

The app spells out its 1RM formula and where your log is saved

SetNote rule sheet

When records can be lost
Settings show ‘Saving in this browser only’ under ‘Where records are saved’. Clearing browser data deletes your records, and for the Home Screen app, deleting the app deletes them.SetNote settings: ‘Where records are saved’
Backup file
‘Export backup’ saves your routines and all records as one JSON file. With ‘Import backup’, anything already there with the same ID is replaced by the backup.SetNote settings: ‘Backup’
Estimated 1RM
Uses the Epley formula, weight × (1 + reps/30). A single rep counts as the weight itself.SetNote app: ‘Est. 1RM’ note
New records
When an exercise's best estimated 1RM today beats your previous best, the summary marks it as a new record. Bodyweight and first-time exercises are left out.SetNote app
Volume
Weight × reps, added up over the sets you checked. Unchecked sets are not saved.SetNote app
Ads
SetNote has no ads.Lumen Lab Privacy Policy

The rest-end sound may stay quiet when your phone is on silent.
