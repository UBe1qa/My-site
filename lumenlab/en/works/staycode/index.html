Staycode rule sheet

QR standard
Codes follow ISO/IEC 18004 Model 2, versions 1 to 40, in byte mode (UTF-8), so accents, Korean and emoji go in as they are.Staycode ‘Standards’
Error correction
A code still reads with about 7% (L), 15% (M), 25% (Q) or 30% (H) of it covered, and adding a logo switches to H and tells you so.DENSO WAVE: Error correction feature · Checked Oct 14, 2026
WiFi format
Networks are stored in the WIFI: format, with a backslash added before any of the five characters \ ; , : " and H:true added for a hidden network.ZXing wiki: Barcode Contents (WiFi format) · Checked Oct 14, 2026
Print size
For close-up scans the code should be at least 2 cm (0.8 in) wide, and as a rule of thumb it scans from about 10 times its width. Sizes in PDF and EPS files measure the pattern without the quiet zone or frame.QR Code Generator blog: Minimum QR code size (rule of thumb) · Checked Oct 14, 2026
Scan check
Contrast between dots and background under 2:1 is ‘No scan’ before the image is even read, and under 4:1 is ‘Check’. ‘Scans’ appears only when what was read matches your content character for character and no other rule is triggered.Staycode guide: QR code with a logo · Checked Oct 14, 2026
Commercial use
The QR code pattern needs no fee or license for commercial use, and a trademark notice is only needed when you use the words “QR Code”.DENSO WAVE: QR Code FAQ (trademark and fees) · Checked Oct 14, 2026

The scan check reads the image again inside your browser, so real results also depend on print quality, light and camera.
