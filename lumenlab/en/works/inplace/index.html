Drop in a file and only the tools that fit it show up

  1. Choose a fileOn the home page, click ‘Choose a file’ or drop in a video, audio, image, PDF, CSV or Excel file.
  2. Pick what to doUnder ‘What do you want to do with it?’ only the tools that open that file appear as cards, and the one you pick opens right there.
  3. Set it and runTo pull the audio out of a video, pick a ‘Format’ and ‘Bitrate’ (192 kbps by default), check the estimated size and press ‘Extract audio’.
  4. Download the resultThe ‘Done’ card shows the new size and how long it took, and ‘Download’ (or ‘Download all (ZIP)’ for several files) saves it to your device.

What it checks and discloses

Files are converted inside your browser and never uploaded. The open-source libraries that do the work (Mediabunny, LAME, libheif, PDF.js, pdf-lib, qpdf, SheetJS and more) are all named on its About page.

When the codecs fit, the streams are copied without re-encoding

Inplace rule sheet

Changing video formats
When the codecs fit the new container, the streams are copied without re-encoding. For MP4 or MOV, VP9 or AV1 video becomes H.264 (or HEVC) and Opus or Vorbis audio becomes AAC, so the file plays on iPhone and Mac.Mediabunny (with the browser’s WebCodecs)
MP3 quality
MP3s are encoded by LAME 3.100 compiled to WebAssembly at 128, 192 or 320 kbps. From a video the default is 192 kbps, and 320 kbps is the highest bitrate the MP3 format allows.@mediabunny/mp3-encoder (LAME 3.100)
HEIC photos
Safari decodes HEIC itself, and in Chrome, Edge and Firefox the page uses libheif compiled to WebAssembly. The capture date and GPS location are not copied to the new file.libheif-js (libheif)
PDF compression
Only photos and scans are re-saved as JPEG (default ‘Balanced’: about 1600 pixels on the long side, quality 70), and the file structure is packed into object streams. If the result isn’t smaller, you get the original back.qpdf documentation: Running qpdf (object streams and stream compression) · Checked Oct 6, 2026
PDF passwords
Files are encrypted with AES-256 as described in the PDF 2.0 specification, so a viewer needs PDF 1.7 extension level 8 support to open them. ‘Also block printing and copying’ is enforced by the viewer, so it is the open password that keeps the content private.qpdf documentation: PDF encryption (user and owner passwords, security restrictions) · Checked Oct 6, 2026
CSV for Excel
Excel reads a CSV as UTF-8 only when it starts with a byte order mark (the bytes EF BB BF), so ‘Excel-friendly (UTF-8 with BOM)’ is on from the start.Microsoft Support: Opening CSV UTF-8 files correctly in Excel (Korean page) · Checked Oct 6, 2026

The practical limit is your device’s memory, so very large videos can fail on phones, and Word, HWP or PowerPoint to PDF and OCR aren’t available yet.
