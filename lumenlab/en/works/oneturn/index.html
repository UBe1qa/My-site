Try it here

Loading

Drag the dial to set the minutes. Once it runs, one turn of the dial matches the time you set.

One tap on ‘5 min’ starts the timer

  1. Tap a presetTap one of the 8 buttons under ‘Tap to start’, from ‘1 min’ to ‘1 hour’, and the timer starts right away.
  2. Type an exact timeEnter hours, minutes and seconds under ‘Set exactly’ and press ‘Start’; the teal band on the dial shows the time left and shrinks back toward 12.
  3. Test the soundPick ‘Bell’, ‘Beep’ or ‘Soft’ under ‘Sound’ and press ‘Test sound’ to hear it before the timer ends.
  4. Name and add timersGive a timer a name and use ‘Add a timer’ to run more; when one ends, the alert shows its name.

What it checks and discloses

City times come from the IANA time zone database, with the 2026 rule changes for Canada and Morocco applied separately, and the tests compare every city with Python's zoneinfo.

In a background tab it still rings on time, because it sets one timer for the end

Rules and sourcesChecked Oct 14, 2026

City times
Uses the IANA time zone data in your browser. The 2026 rule changes for Canada and Morocco are built in, so those cities stay right on older browsers, and the world clock says when your browser's data is out of date.IANA change log
Ringing in a background tab
In a tab hidden for over 5 minutes, Chrome may check timers that keep re-arming themselves only once a minute, so Oneturn works out the time left from the end time.Chrome for Developers: Heavy throttling of chained JS timers beginning in Chrome 88
Screen off or another app
When the screen turns off or you switch apps, the browser may freeze the page, and no timer runs while it is frozen.Chrome for Developers: Page Lifecycle API
Keep screen on
‘Keep screen on’ works from Chrome 84, Safari 16.4, Safari on iOS 18.4 and Firefox 126. Other browsers show ‘This browser cannot keep the screen on’.MDN: Screen Wake Lock API, browser compatibility
What stays on your device
Timer names and end times, stopwatch laps, alarms, cities and settings are kept in this browser only and never sent to a server.Oneturn Privacy Policy (the six things it stores)

It cannot ring once the tab is closed or the device sleeps, so use your phone's clock app for an alarm you cannot miss.
