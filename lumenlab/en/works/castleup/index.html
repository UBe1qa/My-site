Castle Up!

Idle defense for phones · Android first

Play-testing

What it is ↗

An idle defense: heroes on every floor fight on their own, and every 15 waves you break a boss by hitting its weakness order.

  • 50heroes (30 recruits + 20 boss)
  • 20bosses, one every 15 waves
  • 1, 4, 8 hexpeditions while closed

Preview

Real game screens. The game is still in development, so they may change before launch.

Learn more

  • One hero per floorEvery promotion adds a floor
  • Fights on, explores offAuto skills, auto replay and expeditions
  • 20 bossesEach one has a weak element
  • QuestionsLaunch, payment and ad plans

What we disclose

Screens and numbers reflect the game in development and may change before launch. The logo, heroes, bosses, castles and backgrounds were made with an AI tool (Higgsfield); in-game character motion comes from AI video (Kling). Recruiting heroes is a random draw, and the odds are shown in the game.
