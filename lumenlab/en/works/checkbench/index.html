What it checks and discloses

Each verdict shows its threshold (30 ms for keyboard chatter, 50 ms for double-clicks and so on), and what a web page cannot see, such as a mouse's true USB polling rate, is stated plainly.

If your count stops at six keys, the basic USB report format may be why

Rules and sources

Mic and camera access
Access is requested through getUserMedia only when you press a button. If it fails, the browser's error tells the page whether the site is blocked, your system settings block it or no device was found.MDN: MediaDevices.getUserMedia() · Checked Oct 10, 2026
Mic level
Measured with echo cancelling, noise suppression and auto volume switched off on request. On a scale where 0 dBFS is the loudest value, passing −45 dBFS counts as picking up sound, and a signal cut off at the top is flagged as too loud.About Checkbench
Refresh rate
Timed with requestAnimationFrame over 120 frames, using the median gap. Within 1% of a common rate it says ‘about’ that rate, and if fewer than 75% of frames arrive on time it gives no number.About Checkbench
Keys held at once
Every key down at the same moment counts, modifiers included. The basic USB keyboard report holds six keys besides the modifiers.USB-IF, Device Class Definition for Human Interface Devices (HID) 1.11 · Checked Oct 10, 2026
Gamepad sticks
A resting average under 5% counts as centered and 15% or more as drifting. Gamepad API values are shown unfiltered, so games that ignore small offsets near the center may not show slight drift.Microsoft Learn: Getting Started With XInput in Windows applications (Dead Zone) · Checked Oct 10, 2026
What stays on your device
Mic audio, recordings, camera video and photos are handled inside the tab and never uploaded. The browser keeps only a few settings, such as your keyboard layout and the double-typing and double-click limits.Checkbench Privacy Policy

The pass marks are Checkbench's own rules, not an official standard, and a meeting app may clean up your mic, so a call can sound different.
