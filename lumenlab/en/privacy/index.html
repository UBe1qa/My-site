Lumen Lab Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026. How Lumen Lab handles personal information on lumenlab.page and its web tool sites.

Summary

  • There are no accounts or logins. Lumen Lab does not collect your name, email address or phone number on these sites.
  • Text, numbers, dates, name lists, files, microphone audio and camera video you use in the tools are processed in your browser only and are never sent to a Lumen Lab server.
  • The only personal information Lumen Lab receives directly is an email you choose to send us (your address and the message).
  • The web tool sites show Google AdSense ads, and ad companies such as Google may use cookies (section 4). lumenlab.page itself has no ads.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.
  • Every site has cookie-free visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, section 3).

3. What stays on your device, and visit statistics

Browser storage

To make the next visit easier, the sites keep a few settings in your browser’s storage (localStorage). Nothing there is sent to a server.

  • lumenlab.page the language you picked by closing the language suggestion or pressing the language button (lumen:langbar), and the language chosen on the app support pages (lumen:lang)
  • Web tool sites some tools keep settings or recent results. Each tool lists exactly what in its own policy (section 1).

Clearing site data in your browser removes all of it.

Visit statistics

Cloudflare, which hosts the sites, adds a small script for visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). It collects things like the page address, the previous page address, how long the page took to load and the browser type, and it does not use cookies. Nothing you enter in a tool is part of it. Lumen Lab only sees the totals in Cloudflare’s dashboard (visits, most-viewed pages and so on).

Server logs

To deliver pages, Cloudflare processes connection details such as your IP address and may keep basic logs for a short time to run the service securely. Some tools load files such as fonts from a public CDN (for example jsDelivr), which also receives your IP address like any web request. Neither receives what you enter in a tool.

4. Advertising and cookies (web tool sites)

The web tool sites show ads through Google AdSense. lumenlab.page and the app support and privacy pages have no ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Because of ads, third parties such as Google may place and read cookies on your browser and may use web beacons (tiny invisible images) to collect information.
  • See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Information used for personalized ads

Collected by
Google (AdSense)
What
Browsing data such as ad cookie IDs, the pages viewed and when, ads seen or clicked, and browser and device details
How
Collected automatically by the ad code, through cookies and web beacons, when a web tool page opens
Why
To show ads, choose ads that match your interests and measure how ads perform
How long
As set out in Google’s Privacy Policy
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings, and opt out of other vendors’ personalized ads at aboutads.info.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The tools keep working, and you may see non-personalized ads.

Ad data is collected and processed by Google directly. Lumen Lab does not receive or store it; we only see AdSense’s totals (impressions, earnings and so on).

5. Sharing, service providers and transfers abroad

Lumen Lab does not sell your personal information or hand it to third parties; ad cookies are set by Google directly (section 4). We use the service providers below. Both are US companies, so information may be processed on servers outside Korea, including in the United States.

Cloudflare, Inc.
Hosting and visit statistics, and forwarding mail sent to contact@lumenlab.page to our mailbox. Data involved: connection details, the statistics in section 3, and the address and content of emails you send. Privacy contact: privacyquestions@cloudflare.com
Google LLC
Keeping your emails in our mailbox (Gmail). Data involved: the address and content of emails you send. Privacy contact: the contact link in Google’s Privacy Policy

Data is encrypted in transit and kept as described in section 2. If you don’t want your email transferred abroad, please don’t email us; we won’t be able to reply in that case. Ad data (section 4) is collected by Google directly, not passed on by us.

6. Deletion

  • When the retention period ends or the purpose is met, the information is deleted without delay.
  • Electronic files are deleted so they can’t be recovered. We keep no paper records.
  • Settings in your browser storage are removed when you clear the site’s data.

7. Your rights

  • You (or a legal guardian for a child under 14) can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. Email us and we will act without delay.
  • Information we don’t hold (your browser storage, ad data processed by Google) can be managed in your browser settings and in your Google account and ad settings.
  • The sites are not aimed at children, and we don’t collect anyone’s personal information on them.

8. Security

  • There is no server that receives what you enter in the tools, so none of it builds up on our side.
  • All sites are served over encrypted connections (HTTPS) only.
  • Emails are kept in a mailbox only Lumen Lab can access.
