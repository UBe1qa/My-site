Delete and Reorder PDF Pages

3412→1234

Drop blank or unwanted pages and fix the page order in one go, on your own device.

or drop it here

Accepts: PDF

Converted on this device. Your files are never uploaded.

Remove, restore, move

Each thumbnail has a remove toggle. A removed page is greyed out rather than gone, so one more tap brings it back before you save.

The ← and → buttons move a page one place earlier or later. The new PDF holds the kept pages in the order shown on screen.

Pages are copied, not re-rendered

Kept pages are copied into the new file with their content as it is, so text stays selectable and images keep their quality. Your original file isn't changed.

Links or bookmarks that pointed to removed pages won't have anywhere to go in the new file.

Typical uses

Removing the blank pages a double-sided scan leaves behind, taking out a page with personal details before you send a document, or moving an appendix to the end.

Questions

How is this different from Split PDF?

Split PDF works from typed page ranges and can make several files. This page shows thumbnails, which helps when you need to see the pages, and always makes one PDF.

Can I add pages from another PDF?

Not here. Combine the files with Merge PDF first, then remove or move pages in the result.

What happens to the pages I remove?

They're left out of the new file. The original PDF on your device still has them.
