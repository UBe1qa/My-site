    Before you rely on this alarm

    Keep this tab open

    This page plays the alarm. If you close the tab or the computer goes to sleep, it will not ring. For a wake-up alarm you cannot miss, use your phone's clock app.

    Missed alarms are flagged

    When you come back, any alarm that already passed is marked as missed and kept so you can turn it on again.

    See how long until it rings

    The dial shades the time from now to the next alarm, and the list says things like "in 6 h 12 min". Past 12 hours the dial shows a full turn.

    Stop it or snooze

    While it rings, a bar appears at the top with Stop sound and Snooze 5 min. An alarm turns off after it rings once.

    Questions people ask

    Will it ring if my computer goes to sleep?

    No. Sleep, closing the tab or quitting the browser stops the page. When you come back, missed alarms are flagged.

    Can it ring every day?

    Not yet. An alarm turns off after it rings once; switch it back on in the list and it rings at that time next.

    Why is there no sound?

    Use Test sound and check the volume and silent mode. Browsers may block sound until you tap the page once, so after a refresh tap the page once.

    How many alarms can I set?

    Up to 12. Give each one a label and it shows when it rings.
